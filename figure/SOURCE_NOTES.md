# Figure sources and scope

These figures were prepared for the HAKCC public disclosure under Zhenhai He's direction, with AI-assisted drafting. They describe the recorded platform snapshot and its pedagogical interpretation. Editable draw.io sources are included so the diagrams can be inspected and revised.

Local reference diagrams informed visual organization: grouped mechanisms, restrained color coding, modular boxes, and directional relationships. The reference originals are excluded. The HAKCC diagrams redraw this platform's own structure; borrowed visual conventions do not establish authorship of the reference works or ownership of a general diagram style.

For functional support, see [implementation evidence](../write/IMPLEMENTATION_EVIDENCE.en.md). The diagrams simplify technical details and access exceptions; the source and documentation specify actual permissions. In particular, Rise-above publication is allowed for the room creator or authorized course staff, and AI availability is conditional.

KB and KF are acknowledged intellectual sources. Notes, Views, Build-on, Scaffolds, and Rise-above follow established KF precedents. See [Scardamalia (2003)](https://ikit.org/fulltext/2003_KFAdvances.htm) and the [full theoretical references](../write/THEORETICAL_FOUNDATIONS.en.md).
